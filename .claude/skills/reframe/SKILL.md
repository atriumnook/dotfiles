name: reframe
description: Reframe a task with radically greater ambition.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Reframe $ARGUMENTS. Use whichever of Altitude, Depth, and Range fit, and say in a line why any you skip doesn't.
A request is usually sized to what the requester believed was possible, not to what you and this environment can do; find the version worth doing.
- Altitude: Ask what should happen once this task is done, and aim at that outcome. Name the access and tools (data, an environment to reproduce in) that outcome depends on.
- Depth: Set a specific target well past what seems reasonable and state it as an ordinary requirement. Work as a top practitioner would: their instruments, the rawest data, and a test of the premise against it. Name the evidence, from a source other than you, that would show the result is right — ideally a check that can be run.
- Range: Restate the task by what it must accomplish rather than what it produces, then bring in practitioners from fields it wouldn't normally involve. Borrow what they do, not how they sound.
Propose the most ambitious version you can defend — deeper or higher, not merely bigger — rather than an incremental improvement. Preserve hard constraints, distinguish hypotheses from proven feasibility, and do not execute.
